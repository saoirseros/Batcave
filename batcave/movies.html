Batman: The Movie (1966)

PG-13 • 1h 45m • Family/Action

Batman and Robin discover a secret invention that the evil villains plan to use to dehydrate the entire population in exchange for ransom. The duo must find a way to stop them and save the world.

Director: Leslie H. Martinson Stars: Adam West, Burt Ward, Cesar Romero

Batman (1989)

PG-13 • 2h 6m • Action/Crime

Batman, a masked vigilante from Gotham City, fights against evil to keep its citizens safe. He must battle Jack Napier, who turns into the Joker and threatens to take over Gotham City.

Director: Tim Burton Stars: Michael Keaton, Jack Nicholson, Kim Basinger

Batman Returns(1992)

PG-13 • 2h 6m • Action/Sci-fi

The evil Penguin wants to take control of Gotham City and uses Max Shreck to reach his goal. Meanwhile, Batman does everything in his power to stop the Penguin while Catwoman has an agenda of her own.

Director: Tim Burton Stars: Michael Keaton, Michelle Pfeiffer, Danny DeVito

Batman Forever (1995)

PG-13 • 2h 1m • Action/Sci-fi

Batman must stop Two-Face, who seeks revenge from him after a courtroom accident leaves him disfigured on one side, and The Riddler, who wants to drain information from all the brains in Gotham.

Director: Joel Schumacher Stars: Val Kilmer, Jim Carrey, Tommy Lee Jones, Chris O'Donnel

Batman & Robin (1997)

PG-13 • 2h 5m • Action/Sci-fi

The friendship of Batman and Robin is threatened when Poison Ivy puts the latter under her spell. She and Bane later join forces with Mr Freeze to turn humankind into mutant plants.

Director: Joel Schumacher Stars: George Clooney, Uma Thurman, Alicia Silverstone, Chris O'Donnel

Batman Begins (2005)

PG-13 • 2h 20m • Action/Crime

After witnessing his parents' death, billionaire Bruce Wayne learns the art of fighting to confront injustice. When he returns to Gotham as Batman, he must stop a secret society that intends to destroy the city.

Director: Christopher Nolan Stars: Christian Bale, Michael Caine, Ken Watanabe

The Dark Knight (2008)

PG-13 • 2h 32m • Action/Crime

When a menace known as the Joker wreaks havoc and chaos on the people of Gotham, Batman, James Gordon and Harvey Dent must work together to put an end to the madness.

Director: Christopher Nolan Stars: Christian Bale, Heath Ledger, Aaron Eckhart

The Dark Knight Rises (2012)

PG-13 • 2h 44m • Action/Thriller

Bane, an imposing terrorist, attacks Gotham City and disrupts its eight-year-long period of peace. This forces Bruce Wayne to come out of hiding and don the cape and cowl of Batman again.

Director: Christopher Nolan Stars: Christian Bale, Tom Hardy, Anne Hathaway

Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice (2016)

R • 2h 31m • Action/Sci-fi

Batman is manipulated by Lex Luthor to fear Superman. Superman´s existence is meanwhile dividing the world and he is framed for murder during an international crisis. The heroes clash and force the neutral Wonder Woman to reemerge.

Director: Zack Snyder Stars: Ben Affleck, Henry Cavill, Amy Adams

Suicide Squad (2016)

PG-13 • 2h 3m • Action/Fantasy

A secret government agency recruits some of the most dangerous incarcerated super-villains to form a defensive task force. Their first mission: save the world from the apocalypse.

Director: David Ayer Stars: Will Smith, Jared Leto, Margot Robbie

Justice League (2017)

PG-13 • 2h • Action/Adventure

Steppenwolf and his Parademons return after eons to capture Earth. However, Batman seeks the help of Wonder Woman to recruit and assemble Flash, Cyborg and Aquaman to fight the powerful new enemy.

Director: Zack Snyder Stars: Ben Affleck, Henry Cavill, Gal Gadot, Jason Momoa, Ezra Miller

The Lego Batman Movie (2017)

PG • 1h 44m • Family/Comedy

A cooler-than-ever Bruce Wayne must deal with the usual suspects as they plan to rule Gotham City, while discovering that he has accidentally adopted a teenage orphan who wishes to become his sidekick.

Director: Chris McKay Stars: Will Arnett, Michael Cera, Rosario Dawson

Joker (2019)

R • 2h 2m • Thriller/Crime

Arthur Fleck, a party clown and a failed stand-up comedian, leads an impoverished life with his ailing mother. However, when society shuns him and brands him as a freak, he decides to embrace the life of chaos in Gotham City.

Director: Todd Phillips Stars: Joaquin Phoenix, Robert De Niro, Zazie Beetz

The Batman (2022)

PG-13 • 2h 56m • Action/Crime

When a sadistic serial killer begins murdering key political figures in Gotham, the Batman is forced to investigate the city's hidden corruption and question his family's involvement.

Director: Matt Reeves Stars: Robert Pattinson, Zoë Kravitz, Jeffrey Wright

Joker: Folie A Deux (2024)

PG-13 • 2h 18m • Musical/Crime

Struggling with his dual identity, failed comedian Arthur Fleck stumbles upon true love while incarcerated at Arkham State Hospital. Awaiting trial for the murder of five people, Fleck soon discovers the music that's always been inside him

Director: Todd Phillips Stars: Joaquin Phoenix, Lady Gaga, Harry Lawtey
